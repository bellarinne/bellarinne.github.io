Behind the designs is a writer, actually.

Studying English Literature made me a strong storyteller. I love writing compelling user narratives that demonstrate the value of my designs.

Since starting my UX career in 2022, I’ve learned to prioritize a design process that is founded on empathy — designing products that are, of course, visually appealing, and also consider one’s emotional journey. I believe good experience design meets users wherever they are.

Besides design, I’m an avid illustrator and cartoonist, and pursuing a Master’s in Library and Information Science, with a concentration in User Experience, at Pratt Institute.

I also enjoy reading about Romantic and Modernist poets.
